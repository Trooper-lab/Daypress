# Code of Conduct

Daypress adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel) and whenever someone represents the project in public.

## In short

- Be welcoming and respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes and learn from them.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, trolling, sexualized language or imagery, and publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, email **[billy@trooper.es](mailto:billy@trooper.es)**. Every report will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting will be respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's [Enforcement Guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines). Depending on the severity, the consequence is a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the project's spaces.
